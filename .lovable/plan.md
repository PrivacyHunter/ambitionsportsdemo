## Design System
- **Theme:** Dark mode by default.
- **Background:** Deep Obsidian/Slate (`#0f172a`).
- **Accents:** Electric Neon Cyan (`#00f3ff`), Neon Lime (`#39ff14`).
- **Typography:** Bold, modern sans-serif.
- **Animations:** Framer Motion for transitions and hover effects.

## Routes & Pages
1.  **Home (`/`)**: 
    - Notification bar, sticky navbar, neon emblem logo.
    - 6-banner auto-playing hero slider.
    - Auto-scrolling featured products.
    - Product grid with neon badges and hover zoom.
    - Facilities & Work Process sections.
    - Worldwide shipping section with partner logos.
    - Custom sports categories layout.
    - Testimonials & Footer with integrated inquiry form.

2.  **About (`/about`)**:
    - Company history, mission, vision.
    - Categories overview for Sportswear and Activewear.
    - Bulk order inquiry form with file upload support.

3.  **Sportswear (`/sportswear`)**:
    - "Performance Sportswear" hero banner.
    - Product grid with spec sheet request options.

4.  **Activewear (`/activewear`)**:
    - "Custom Activewear & Gym Gear" hero banner.
    - Catalog for gym, compression, and fitness apparel.

5.  **Casual Wear (`/casual-wear`)**:
    - "Custom Casual & Streetwear" hero banner.
    - Catalog for t-shirts, polos, varsity jackets.

6.  **Contact (`/contact`)**:
    - Contact details + WhatsApp quick button.
    - Live Map placeholder.
    - Comprehensive inquiry form.

## Technical Details
- Use `lucide-react` for iconography.
- Use `framer-motion` for animations.
- Implement responsive layouts using Tailwind CSS.
- Mock product data for initial catalog views.
- Shared components: `Navbar`, `Footer` (with form), `HeroBanner`, `ProductCard`.
